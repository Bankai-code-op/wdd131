The Beat-Making Process

Every producer works a little differently, but most beats come together in the same seven steps. Work through them in order, and check each step off as you finish it. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can leave and pick up where you stopped.

    Tips for Your First Beat

    • Aim to finish a simple beat rather than perfect one. Finishing teaches you more than polishing.
    • Listen to a song you like and compare your drums and bass to it as you work.
    • Take short breaks. Your ears tire, and a fresh listen often shows what to fix.
